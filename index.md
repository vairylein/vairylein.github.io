## About Me

<img class="profile-picture" src="vhpicture.jpg">

Hello! Hallo! Halo! I am Vanessa Aisyahsari Hanschke (Pronouns: she/her), Senior Research Associate in Explainable Artificial Intelligence at the University of Bristol. Previously, I was Associate Lecturer in Human-Computer Interaction at the UCL Interaction Centre. I completed my PhD with the [Interactive Artificial Intelligence CDT](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/cdt/interactive-ai/) at the University of Bristol under the supervision of
[Dr. Paul Marshall](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/people/person/Paul-Marshall-36d4dfa7-1ec3-4168-9226-130e47809b65/) and [Professor Kenton O'Hara](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/persons/kenton-ohara).


## Research Interest

I am currently exploring how we can improve current AI explainability tools to be more useful and actionable for non-technical stakeholders. My wider research interests include:

* Ethical literacy for data and AI practitioners
* Data and AI ethics, responsible AI, explainable AI
* AI literacy for non-experts
* Speculative design and storytelling methods
