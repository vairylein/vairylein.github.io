## Research Interest

I am currently exploring how developers can be encouraged to reflect on the ethical aspects of their data and AI practise. My wider research interests include:

* Ethical literacy for data and AI practitioners
* Data and AI ethics, responsible AI, explainable AI
* AI literacy for non-experts
* Speculative design and storytelling methods
